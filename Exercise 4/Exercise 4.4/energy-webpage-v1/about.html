About Us

This website was created to provide useful information about appliance energy consumption in the Australian market.

It focuses on common household technologies, especially televisions, and explains how energy-efficient choices can help reduce electricity usage.

The aim of this website is to help users better understand energy performance standards, energy rating labels, and the role of smart technology in modern homes. By learning about these topics, consumers can make better decisions when purchasing appliances.

Energy efficiency is important because it can lower household electricity costs and reduce negative effects on the environment. Choosing efficient appliances and using smart features responsibly can support a more sustainable lifestyle.

This website was developed as an educational project to demonstrate the use of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in building a simple interactive website.
